# Brewline Coffee Brand Guidelines (synthetic demo data, fictional brand)

G-01 | Voice: Write in a warm, calm and confident voice, like a knowledgeable barista. Avoid slang and hype.
G-02 | Punctuation and emphasis: Use at most one exclamation mark per email and never write words in all capitals for emphasis.
G-03 | Preferred terminology: Use the approved glossary. Describe our pricing as affordable or great value, never cheap.
G-04 | Claims: Every comparative, superlative, ranking or numeric claim must match an approved fact in the fact sheet. If no approved fact exists, remove or reword the claim.
G-05 | Product facts: Prices, launch dates, roast levels and sourcing must match the current fact sheet exactly.
G-06 | Accessibility: Every image needs alt text between 5 and 125 characters, and text placed on an image needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1.
G-07 | Health language: Never describe coffee as addictive or imply medical benefits.
G-08 | Image colour: Images use the brand palette (Espresso #3B2314, Crema #F4E9D8, Copper #B87333) with neutral greys and white. Other colours may cover only a small part of an image.
G-09 | Logo use: Where the logo appears it is shown whole, at least 15% of the image width, with clear space of at least half its height on every side.
